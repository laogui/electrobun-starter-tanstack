### Hero

**Your thinking isn't linear. Your notes shouldn't be either.**

GhostBoard is a spatial canvas for researchers and writers who need to see the whole picture — and the hidden connections within it.

---

### The Problem

You've read thirty papers. Highlighted two hundred passages. Written thousands of words in notes. And still, something is missing.

The structure. The pattern. The *aha* that only comes when you can see everything at once and feel how the pieces relate.

Linear notes are great for capturing. But thinking — real, deep, connective thinking — needs space.

---

### The Product

GhostBoard gives your ideas a place to spread out.

An infinite canvas where every note, image, and link is a card you can move, connect, and group. Zoom out to see the forest. Zoom in to read the leaves. Open a card and it fills your screen — no context switch, no lost thread.

It's not a note-taking app. It's the table where your thinking happens.

---

### Three Things That Make It Different

**Space is the point, not a feature.**
Most tools bolt a canvas onto a notes app and call it a day. GhostBoard was built from the ground up around spatial interaction — infinite zoom, smooth element transitions, and two distinct ways to organize: open Groups for visible layout, stacked Folders for hidden depth. The canvas isn't a view of your notes. The canvas *is* your workspace.

**Your files are your files.**
Every note is a Markdown file on your disk. No cloud dependency, no proprietary database, no subscription lock-in. Open them in any editor, version them with Git, back them up however you like. Ten years from now, your notes will still be readable — because they were never trapped inside an app.

**Motion that helps you think.**
Cards don't pop open — they expand from where they sit on the canvas, so you never lose your spatial context. Folders spread their contents with a gentle animation that makes the hierarchy feel physical. These aren't decorative flourishes. They're the difference between a tool that feels like a spreadsheet and one that feels like a workshop.

---

### Who It's For

GhostBoard is for the kind of person who spreads papers across a desk, draws lines between paragraphs, and rearranges sticky notes on a wall — except digital, and without the mess.

Independent researchers piecing together literature. Writers structuring a book or a long essay. Strategists mapping out a complex problem. Anyone whose thinking is too rich for a list.
